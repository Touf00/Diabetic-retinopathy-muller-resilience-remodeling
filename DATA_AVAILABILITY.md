# Data availability

All primary datasets analyzed in this study are public or published supplementary data.

- **GSE328966** — mouse retinal single-cell RNA-seq at 5 and 15 days of diabetes; source study: Rajasekar et al., *Biomolecules* (2026), DOI 10.3390/biom16040614.
- **GSE313176** — longitudinal mouse diabetic retina bulk RNA-seq and 3-month single-cell RNA-seq; source study: Corano Scheri et al., *Life Science Alliance*, DOI 10.26508/lsa.202503434.
- **Human 2026 retinal single-cell atlas** — 20 eyes from 13 donors across NON, DM and DR; source study: Lin et al., *Human Molecular Genetics* (2026), DOI 10.1093/hmg/ddag069; corresponding Figshare files are enumerated in the analysis notebook.
- **GSE160306** — human post-mortem retinal bulk RNA-seq across diabetic retinopathy stages and retinal regions; Becker et al., *Scientific Reports* (2021), DOI 10.1038/s41598-021-88698-3.
- **GSE290024** — human retinal organoid RNA-seq under 25 mM versus 17-17.5 mM glucose; Polešovská et al., *Cell Death & Disease* (2025), DOI 10.1038/s41419-025-08244-1.
- **Lin et al. 2025 Supplementary Table S2** — rat Müller cell 1/2/3-month STZ-vs-control differential-expression tables, Figshare DOI-linked supplement to DOI 10.2337/db24-0199.

Derived locked signatures, ortholog mappings, result tables, and figure-generation code should be archived with the manuscript release (e.g., GitHub + Zenodo) so that the exact analyzed state is citable.
